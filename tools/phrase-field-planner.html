Mosspit Tag Checker for Etsy

Paste your tags (a list or straight from a spreadsheet), check them against Etsy's rules, then copy all 13 into your listing in one paste.

Free tool. It checks character limits and arranges your phrases. It does not predict rankings, traffic or sales, and it cannot know how any marketplace will read your text. You are responsible for what you publish.

You can also paste cells straight from a spreadsheet (a row, a column or a block). Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.

Etsy rules: up to 13 tags of up to 20 characters, using letters, numbers and spaces. ' and - are allowed inside a tag but not at the start. Changing the numbers switches to Custom.
