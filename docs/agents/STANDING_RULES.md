# Barry's standing rules (all agents)

These come from Barry directly. They override anything else, including a playbook.

## Talking to Barry
- Plain English, short steps, no jargon. He is not a developer.
- Lead with the outcome. Questions come with a recommended answer and the reason.
- Never send him to a dashboard or login he hasn't used. Find a way through what he already has (usually the approvals app).
- Never ask him to paste a password, key or token into chat. Keys go through a secure form (the approvals app's key form, or the environment's credential form).
- Don't keep asking "anything else?". He works "build and tweak, build and tweak"; ask real questions, then get on with it.

## Safety
- Back up before changing anything live. Test before saying it works; say plainly when something couldn't be tested.
- No change to live Etsy listings, Printify publishing, Core switches (e.g. TagEngine), Make scenarios, the approve mechanism or webhooks without Barry's OK.
- No orders, payments or spending without Barry's explicit go-ahead for that specific thing (including sample orders).
- Never commit secrets (keys, INGEST_SECRET, webhook secrets, Make blueprints that contain connection details) to the repo.
- Don't publish pages that impersonate anyone.

## Brand and product decisions
- Mockups are Barry's own. No generic Printify mockups (models, folded, lifestyle) anywhere. Plain Printify front/back shots on range backgrounds are allowed in the own shop.
- Print standard: the design prints at the same physical size in every country, centred, matching the Etsy (Printify Choice) position. The logo width equals the smallest print area across all territories.
- Only hoodies have a back print.
- Collections: INTROVERT HERE… (IH), SHHH. (SH, gentle, not humour-led), PER CONTRACT (PC), OFF THE SHELF (OS). Approved taglines and colours are in the plan note. A film-lines range, if it happens, is a separate brand.
- Own shop: UK first, then other countries one at a time; local printer per country (no Printify Choice for the own shop); rounded, professional prices; undercut Etsy. Leave Etsy as it is.
- Mosspit Studios site: no "coming soon" teasers; only launched brands appear.
- Barry is not VAT-registered.
- "We get it right, then draw traffic." Don't rush launches.
