# Watchtower briefing: Monday 16 November 2026

First Watchtower report, so this one covers changes from the last few months that still matter now. Not legal or tax advice; each item says who to check with.

## Headline
Etsy now says overseas sellers must pay US import duty upfront on parcels to the US. We should check that our US orders are printed in the US, which would mean the rule doesn't touch us. Also: Etsy charges no transaction fee on "Share & Save" orders until 4 January.

## Act on this

**1. Etsy's US import duty rule (in force since 9 July 2026)**
- **What changed:** If a seller outside the US posts a parcel into the US, Etsy now requires the import duty to be paid upfront ("Delivered Duty Paid") and built into the price. If it isn't, the order loses Etsy Purchase Protection and the seller can be charged the buyer's duty and fees.
- **What it means for us:** Probably nothing, as long as our US orders go through Printify Choice to a US printer. A parcel posted inside the US pays no import duty. Printify says products printed in the US generally avoid these tariffs. The risk is any listing where a US order could be printed in the UK and posted across.
- **Proposed change:** The Printify agent looks at the last few US orders in Printify and confirms the printer was in the US. Read-only, no changes. If any were printed in the UK, we come back to you with options before touching listings.
- **Who to check with:** Printify support (whether Choice ever sends US orders from outside the US) and Etsy's Help Centre.

**2. No Etsy transaction fee on Share & Save orders (until 9am US Eastern, 4 January 2027)**
- **What changed:** Etsy normally gives back 4% of the 6.5% transaction fee when a buyer arrives through a link you shared yourself. Until 4 January it gives back the full 6.5%. Joining is free (Shop Manager).
- **What it means for us:** Sales that come from our own Pinterest, Instagram and Facebook posts could cost us no transaction fee over Christmas. Links in Etsy Messages or in listing descriptions don't count, and neither do orders where the buyer's last click was an Offsite Ad. Payment processing and listing fees still apply.
- **Proposed change:** Your decision. **Recommendation: yes, join (it's free)**, then have the Social agent use Share & Save links in posts until 4 January. Changing how the Make approve scenario builds social links would need your OK first, so we'd propose that separately.
- **Who to check with:** Etsy's Share & Save help page for the exact rules.

## Keep an eye on
- **UK VAT:** the 28 October Budget kept the registration threshold at £90,000, so nothing changes for you. From October 2027, marketplaces may have to collect VAT on UK sellers' sales once those sales pass £90,000. Not relevant below that.
- **Making Tax Digital for Income Tax:** from 6 April 2027 it covers sole traders whose *takings* (before costs) were over £30,000 in 2025/26. From April 2028 the line drops to £20,000, based on 2026/27 takings. It will probably catch you at some point, so bring it up with an accountant in the spring.
- **HMRC sales reports:** Etsy reports UK sellers' sales to HMRC once they pass about 30 sales or €2,000 a year. Your tax return should match Etsy's figures, which the Finance agent can check in December. A report doesn't mean tax is owed.
- **EU charges on parcels from outside the EU:** there has been a €3 customs duty on small parcels since 1 July 2026, and a €2 handling fee since 1 November 2026. These only hit parcels posted from the UK into the EU. Printify Choice prints EU orders inside the EU, so most of our orders should avoid them. Worth knowing before any own-shop expansion into the EU.
- **UK low-value imports:** the UK is ending duty-free entry for parcels under £135 by March 2029 at the latest. This doesn't affect UK-printed goods.
- **UK product safety overhaul (Product Regulation and Metrology Act):** the consultations closed on 23 June. Marketplaces like Etsy are likely to get new duties to check sellers and products. We're waiting for the government's response. No action yet.
- **US de minimis:** still suspended, and the rules were made permanent in June. This matches the plan for the US shop (use a US printer).
- **Printify:** a $0.40 surcharge on US-delivered orders runs until 17 January 2027. There's no news of the Gildan 5000, 2400, 18000 or 18500 being dropped. Printify's 2026 Christmas order deadlines weren't found; last year's US express cut-off was 17 December.
- **Print Clever (our planned UK own-shop printer):** Trustpilot has mixed 2026 reviews, with some complaints about long print delays through Printify. Worth a test order before launch, but only with your go-ahead.
- **Stripe:** no change to UK fees (still about 1.5% + 20p per UK card).
- **Trade marks:** no filing for "Introvert Here" turned up. Several US marks use "introvert" for clothing (e.g. INTROVERT//EXTROVERT, filed 2025), but none is close to our name. If you ever want to protect the name, a UK filing starts at about £205 for one class (fees rose in April 2026). It's optional, and a trade mark attorney or the UK IPO can advise.
- **Etsy rules:** print-on-demand is still allowed under the 9 July seller policy, provided the designs are original and production partners are declared. Etsy is also tightening AI disclosure. Our designs and mockups are our own, so no change is needed, but production partners must stay declared on every listing.

## Market notes
- Etsy's Fall/Winter trend report (14 Oct) puts friend-focused gifts first ("Friendsmas"). "Christmas shirt" climbed from 45th to 16th among Etsy searches.
- "Introvert tshirt" gets roughly 600-700 Etsy searches a month. Rival designs lean on "Not a Hugger", "Nope" and "I should have stayed home" themes.
- Rival introvert hoodies and sweatshirts often show sale prices around $21-24, with "original" prices of $40-50. UK novelty Christmas jumpers sit around £25-30. Our £39.99 sweatshirt and £49.99 hoodie are at the top of that range.
- Etsy took 26.2% of sales value in Q3 (fees plus ads), mostly because sellers spent more on ads. No new fees were announced.
- Since 5 October, Etsy's seller policy says sellers must not steer buyers to pay off Etsy. Keep own-shop links out of Etsy messages and listings.
