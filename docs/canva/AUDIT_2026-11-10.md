# Canva print templates vs Printify print areas (audit, 10 Nov 2026)

Read-only. Measured from the print designs the automation generates from each brand template (Canva element data) and from the print files Printify holds (pixel measurement). All print-area sizes from the Printify catalogue API; 300 dpi assumed (Printify's standard).

## Result: the templates are in good shape
- **Page sizes match Printify Choice exactly** for every garment: tee 3951x4919, long sleeve 4494x5100, sweatshirt 3852x4398, hoodie front 3531x2352, hoodie back 3852x3210.
- **The logo fits every country's print area at every size.** Narrowest areas are Canada (Print Geek) tee and long sleeve: 304.8 mm at L+. The logo frame is about 290 mm wide on IH and SH, so it fits with about 7 mm to spare each side. Small sizes scale down the same way in every country.
- **The logo is the same physical width on tee, long sleeve and sweatshirt** (IH 289.7-289.8 mm), so the eyeballing was very accurate.

## Small fixes found
| Template | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| PER CONTRACT T-shirt (L001) | Logo 2.6 mm left of centre | Move the logo 31 px right (page px) |
| PER CONTRACT logo width | 294 / 296 / 297 mm across long sleeve / tee / sweatshirt (IH is identical everywhere) | Optional: standardise to one width |
| Gag text right margin | Text ends 1.0 mm inside the logo's right edge on IH tee/LS, 2.8 mm on IH sweatshirt, 4.3-4.7 mm on PC long sleeve/sweatshirt | Optional: align to one margin |
| SHHH. logo height | SHHH. logo starts ~20 mm from the top of the print area; IH ~7.5 mm, PC ~5 mm | Check this is intended |
| PER CONTRACT T-shirt and hoodie | Logo layer not locked (others are) | Lock to prevent accidental moves |

Not measured to the millimetre: hoodie front pocket logos (IH 1400 px wide; SHHH. and PER CONTRACT badges ~706 px, different artwork by design).
