# Mosspit Studios mascot: character brief

Working name: **TBD** (ideas: Rupert, Tarquin, Giles, Chad; surname "Cheeks" optional).
Status: brief written 9 Nov 2026. No artwork yet.

## The joke

A smartarse, literally: a peach, which everyone reads as a backside, dressed as a smug 1980s yuppie banker.
He's the corporate world Mosspit's brands make fun of: the manager behind the memos, the reason
Elspeth is outside on her cigarette. Smart suit, and still an arse.

## Look

- **Body:** a ripe peach (soft orange to rosy pink, faint fuzz). The cleft is clearly a peach cleft, so the "arse" read is instant, but cheeky rather than crude: no anatomy, nothing that would get flagged on Etsy or Pinterest.
- **Face:** on the front of the peach. Smug: half-lidded eyes, one eyebrow slightly raised, a tight self-satisfied smirk. "Per my last email" energy. Simple cartoon features.
- **Outfit, 1980s City/Wall Street:**
  - navy **pinstripe** double-breasted suit jacket
  - **blue shirt with an oversized white contrast collar** (the key detail)
  - **red pocket square**, puffed
  - optional: red braces, slim tie, chunky gold watch
- **Hair (optional):** a slicked-back swoosh of peach fuzz, or the peach's stalk and leaf styled like a gelled quiff.
- **Arms and legs:** thin cartoon limbs, white gloves or simple hands. Polished black shoes.
- **Props (pick one per pose):** a brick-sized 80s mobile phone, a takeaway coffee, a Filofax, a clipboard, finger guns.

## Style

- Bold, clean cartoon with thick even outlines and flat colour (two or three shades at most). It must work small (icon size) and in one colour (black on white, white on black).
- Simple shapes, no fine texture: it has to print cleanly on shirts and mugs and survive being 32 pixels wide.
- Palette: peach and pink, navy suit, white collar, one red accent. Nothing else competes.

## Avoid (keeps clear of Mr Peanut and others)

- No top hat, monocle, cane, spats or tailcoat. No Planters blue and yellow.
- No likeness of a real person or film character. Don't prompt with actor or character names.
- No crude anatomy, no nudity, no swearing on the mascot itself.

## Poses to generate

1. **Logo pose:** standing three-quarter view, one hand in pocket, other doing finger guns. Smug.
2. **Icon:** head and shoulders only (face, collar, pocket square) in a circle.
3. **On the phone:** brick phone to ear, mid-"let's circle back".
4. **Disapproving:** arms folded, one eyebrow up (for "not endorsed by management" uses).
