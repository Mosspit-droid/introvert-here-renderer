# Brief for the social media thread: the website launch

From the shop thread, 24 Nov 2026. Barry wants social media as automated as possible ("a necessary evil").
Nothing gets posted without his OK.

## The site

- **introverthere.com**, launching **Monday 30 November**. Until then the domain isn't live and the only copy is a
  private preview: don't post the preview link.
- Two halves: **the Shop** (all the Etsy designs, cheaper than Etsy, plus some designs out on the site before Etsy) and
  **the Escape Room** (a hub for introverts). Front-page strap: "Everything you need to be left the hell alone."
- Escape Room contents (all at introverthere.com/uk/escape-room/...): a warning gate ("Abandon hope…"), a cosy room
  you tap around in, **Look Busy** (fake busy phone screen), **the Excuse Generator** (40 absurd excuses, very
  shareable), **Spot the Introvert** (find the hidden introvert in a painting; 20 paintings in different art styles, 5 a game;
  has a Share button), **Vote on the next gag** (5% code for voting), **Escape From Aunt Doris** (paper/online escape room, 19 puzzles, Part
  One free, £4.99 to unlock; also a printable booklet for Etsy later, not yet listed).
- **Hoodini** is the mascot: short, hood up, only his nose showing, hands thrust in his hoodie pocket, hunched. He hides
  in seven places on the site (the Hoodini hunt; all seven earns 5% off). Don't explain where he hides.
- Christmas: gift guide at /uk/gifts; order by **12 Dec** for Christmas delivery (5 Dec for long sleeves).
- Note: the Escape Room's **Aunt Doris** (a hug-obsessed aunt) is a different character from the team's **Doris**
  (the knitting T-rex in `characters.md`). Worth keeping apart in posts.

## Automation that comes almost free

- The site publishes a product feed at **introverthere.com/google-products.xml** (every garment, colour and size,
  updated automatically). The same feed can be added as a **catalogue** in **Pinterest** (auto-creates product pins)
  and **Meta Commerce Manager** (Facebook and Instagram shop and product tags). Set these up after launch day.
- The app already posts each new gag's video to Instagram and Facebook via Etsy's copy.
