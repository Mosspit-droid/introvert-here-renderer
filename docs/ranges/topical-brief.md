# Workshop brief: Topical (new spin-off range)

## Background
Introvert Here (Mosspit Studios) is an Etsy shop selling print-on-demand clothing printed with short deadpan jokes ("gags") about introverts. Printify makes and ships every order; designs are built in Canva; the approvals app and Make automations run everything from a spreadsheet called Core.

- **Main range:** Introvert Here (code IH).
- **Spin-off ranges, each separate with its own code:** SHHH. (SH), Per Contract (PC), Off The Shelf (OS).
- **Gag codes:** range code plus number, e.g. IH-00123.
- **Release schedule:** each gag comes on 4 garments (T-shirt, long-sleeve, sweatshirt, hoodie) and releases on a weekly schedule planned years ahead.
- **Prices:** T-shirt £26.99, with free postage built into the price.
- **Etsy tags:** each listing gets 13 tags, chosen by a tag engine: 5 product tags, 5 gag tags, a title tag and 2 seasonal tags.
- **Social media:** approving a new listing's video in the app posts it to Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest.
- Setting up a spin-off is about a week's work including writing the gags; PC and OS took roughly that.

## The idea
A spin-off range for jokes about whatever everyone is talking about this week. The aim is to catch the moment on social media and pull people into the shop.

## Agreed so far
- **Its own range** with its own code, treated separately like SH, PC and OS. It does not sit inside IH.
- **T-shirt only:** cheaper, an impulse buy, and a signpost to the rest of the shop.
- **Made on demand, not on the weekly schedule.** A "create now" button builds the listing straight away (about 20–30 minutes for the system to make it).
- **One tap from Barry before it goes live**, after he has seen the mockup.
- **Counted separately** from the other ranges' gag numbers.
- **Switches off automatically after 6 weeks unless it has sold;** a sale keeps it live.

## To workshop
1. **Identity:** the range name, its two-letter code and number format, and how it relates to the main Introvert Here brand.
2. **Look:** logo or mark, colours, Canva print template and mockup pages, and which T-shirt colours.
3. **Price:** same as other T-shirts, or a different price point?
4. **Etsy:** its own shop section, the listing title pattern, and description text.
5. **Tags:** reserve 2 of the 13 for the topic (e.g. "eurovision 2027")? Who types them?
6. **Risk rules:** trademarks, real people and brand names (Etsy takedowns can strike the shop), plus tragedy and politics. Should the app warn on names? What is never done?
7. **Speed vs care:** is 20–30 minutes fast enough? What happens if Barry isn't around to tap approve?
8. **Social:** caption style, hashtags, whether it posts straight away, and how it fits the planned social media rework.
9. **Lifecycle:** the 6-week switch-off, what "sold" means (any sale?), and whether a seller can be promoted into a permanent range.
10. **Volume:** how many topical gags a month, and the effect on Make credits and Etsy listing fees ($0.20 each).
